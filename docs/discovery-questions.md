# What we need from the practice

In priority order. Items 1 and 2 unblock slice 2.

1. **The form files.** Blank copies of every form, as the original Word or PDF files (not scans
   or photos). Start with the one used most: probably the daily visit note. Confirm they contain
   no patient data.
2. **Two or three example dictations per form,** made up or fully de-identified, spoken the way
   Dr. Enderby would actually say them. These become the acceptance tests.
3. **How the forms are built.** Are checkboxes real Word checkboxes or typed symbols? Are the
   PDFs fillable? (We can tell from the files.)
4. **Devices.** Where will dictation happen: phone, tablet, treatment-room computer? iOS or
   Android? Is there usable Wi-Fi in the treatment rooms?
5. **ChiroTouch.** Plan tier, whether Rheo is enabled, account manager contact, and how a
   document gets attached to a chart by hand today.
6. **People and roles.** Who documents (owner, associate doctor), who may see charts, who files.
7. **Required fields.** For each form, which fields must be addressed before sign-off. Payer or
   state board documentation requirements the practice already follows.
8. **Terminology.** Preferred phrasing, abbreviations, technique names, the practice's exercise
   library names.
9. **State.** Which state the practice is in, for record-retention and scope rules.
10. **Volume.** Visits per day per clinician, and how long notes take today. This is the baseline
    the pilot is measured against.
